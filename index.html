Explorer & mod editor for Crusader Kings III

Read the game.
Write your mod.

CrusaderPope reads the game's own files and the mods you load, and shows them as something you can browse, read and change — without opening a text editor for every little thing.

What it does

Explore the game

  • Every definition in one searchable list — events, decisions, traits, interactions, laws, faiths, cultures, characters, titles, modifiers, scripted effects … with who uses what.
  • A readable Summary: script turned into plain language (“Gains 50 Prestige”), events as a timeline with their options, conditions and effects.
  • Source, References and Graph views for when you need the script itself.
  • 3D portraits of historical characters, built from their DNA, and a model viewer.
  • A 2D and 3D map: realms, cultures and faiths at any bookmark date, with the game's own terrain.
  • The game's own shaders (or a mod's), recompiled for WebGL.
  • Hover previews, pinnable cards, back and forward that remember tab and scroll position.

Work with mods

  • Edit in place, WYSIWYG: the plain-language view is the editor. Click a line to change it; a keyboard-driven picker knows the game's statements, scopes and values, and the script is written for you.
  • Every change is undoable, and edited files are kept neatly formatted.
  • Who is who in an event and what fires it — no more guessing which scope is which character.
  • Localization edited where it shows, with the game's text codes insertable from a list, including examples of what they return.
  • Launcher mods and playsets: what each mod adds, changes or removes, and where mods conflict.
  • Create, pack and unpack mods; playsets written back to the launcher (with backups).
  • Round-trip 3D models and textures with Blender (glTF; no animations yet).

Get it

1

Download

From the releases page: the setup (.exe, no administrator rights needed) or the portable .zip.

2

Start

A short setup finds your Crusader Kings III installation and your mods folder by itself, and lets you pick the graphics quality.

3

Explore & edit

The first start reads the whole game, later ones load from a cache. Pick a mod to edit and change entries right where you read them.

Requirements

  • Windows 10/11 (Linux and macOS are expected to work but not tested yet).
  • A legal copy of Crusader Kings III — the tool reads the base game files and is useless without them.
  • A reasonably strong PC: the full index takes around 2 GB of memory, more with big mods.

Running from source? See INSTALL.md.

Free, forever

CrusaderPope is free and open source, and it will stay that way. Its license (GPL-3.0 with the Commons Clause) forbids selling it — and anyone putting a fork behind a “donation” paywall may always get wet shirt sleeves after washing their hands. Even with short sleeves.

It was built with AI (Anthropic's Claude), openly and plainly — and CrusaderPope itself will never include any AI functions. The whole story is in the README.

CrusaderPope is a fan-made tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by Paradox Interactive. Crusader Kings III and its content belong to Paradox Interactive; the app reads your own copy of the game and does not include any of its files.
