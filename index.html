Explorer & mod editor for Crusader Kings III

Read the game.
Write your mod.

CrusaderPope reads the game's own files and the mods you load, and shows them as something you can browse, read and change — without opening a text editor for every little thing.

What it does

Readable view

Script, turned into plain language

Every entry gets a Summary that reads like the game itself: “Gains 50 Prestige”, “If the character is an adult: …”. Events become a timeline of their text, conditions and options; traits, decisions, interactions and titles show what they do, not how they are written.

  • Events, decisions, traits, interactions, laws, faiths, cultures, characters, titles, modifiers, scripted effects …
  • Conditions shown as “Only happens if”, effects as what they change
  • Game icons and event illustrations right where they belong

Cross-references

Who uses what — one hover away

Every name is a link. Hover it for a preview card; middle-click pins the card so you can follow its links in turn. The References tab shows everything an entry uses and everything that uses it.

  • Back and forward (mouse buttons, Alt+arrows) return to the page, tab and scroll position you left
  • Who is who in an event and what fires it: the on_actions, events and decisions that lead to it
  • One search across 500,000+ entries and localization keys (Ctrl+K)

Expert views

The script, when you want it

Under Expert: the Source exactly as the game has it, with its file and lines, and a Graph of what leads into an entry and where it leads.

  • Open the file in your editor or reveal it in Explorer
  • Graph depth, flow-only and localization filters

3D portraits & galleries

Historical characters, with their faces

Characters are built in 3D from the game's own DNA, genes, clothes and shaders, next to their stats, traits, family and life events.

  • The game's own shaders (or a mod's), recompiled for WebGL
  • Toggle clothing, facial features, morphs and barbershop looks
  • Galleries for every image and 3D model, coats of arms drawn as in the game

Map

The world at any bookmark date

A 2D and 3D map with realms, de jure hegemonies, cultures, faiths and holdings at any date you pick, drawn with the game's terrain, trees and buildings, or as a paper map.

  • Slide through history; every realm, culture and faith listed with its share
  • “Show on map” from any title
  • Mostly a viewer for now — a map editor is for later

Edit in place

The readable view is the editor

Pick a mod to edit, then change entries right where you read them: click a line to change it, add a new one or remove it. A keyboard-driven picker knows the game's statements, scopes and values, and the app writes the Paradox script for you.

  • “Override in <mod>” copies a game entry into your mod; new entries and duplicates work the same way
  • Localization edited where it shows, with text codes and examples of what they return
  • Every change undoable, edited files kept neatly formatted
  • Round-trip models and textures with Blender (glTF; no animations yet)

Mods

Your playsets, and where mods collide

CrusaderPope reads the launcher's mods and playsets and shows exactly what each loaded mod adds, changes or removes, and whether a mod's copy is the same as the game's, a duplicate the game won't pick, or a real conflict.

  • Create mods, manage playsets, pack and unpack
  • Playsets written back to the launcher, with backups
  • Filters for all of it; entries a total conversion removes can be hidden

Get it

1

Download

From the releases page: the setup (.exe, no administrator rights needed) or the portable .zip.

2

Start

A short setup finds your Crusader Kings III installation and your mods folder by itself, and lets you pick the graphics quality.

3

Explore & edit

The first start reads the whole game, later ones load from a cache. Pick a mod to edit and change entries right where you read them.

Requirements

  • Windows 10/11 (Linux and macOS are expected to work but not tested yet).
  • A legal copy of Crusader Kings III — the tool reads the base game files and is useless without them.
  • A reasonably strong PC: the full index takes around 2 GB of memory, more with big mods.

Running from source? See INSTALL.md.

Free, forever

CrusaderPope is free and open source, and it will stay that way. Its license (GPL-3.0 with the Commons Clause) forbids selling it — and anyone putting a fork behind a “donation” paywall may always get wet shirt sleeves after washing their hands. Even with short sleeves.

It was built with AI (Anthropic's Claude), openly and plainly — and CrusaderPope itself will never include any AI functions. The whole story is in the README.
